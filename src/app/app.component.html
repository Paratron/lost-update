When systems fair, in tandem run,
A single state should merge as one.
But here a mischief hides within,
A lost update, a lurking sin.
Fair worker threads toil night and day,
Yet silent writes do lose their way.
O wicked race that causes plight,
A vanished change, gone from our sight.
Out, out, brief state, thou ghostly ghost,
Thy fleeting update, now we've lost!
To fix this bug, brave dev must dare,
To guard thy state with utmost care.
